# Code of conduct

Short version: be decent, assume good faith, and keep critique about the work.

## Expected

- Say what you mean plainly; a rough sentence beats silence.
- Assume the other person is trying. Ask before assuming they were careless.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change for reasons outside the diff.
- Credit people whose work or report you build on.

## Not welcome

- Personal attacks, pile-ons, or tone policing as a substitute for an argument.
- Harassment of any kind, in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Posting someone's private information without asking.

## Enforcement

A maintainer may edit, hide or split a thread, and may close a contribution that
keeps missing the above. Anything unresolved goes to the maintainer's email on
their GitHub profile. There is no committee; the aim is a working room, not a
court.
